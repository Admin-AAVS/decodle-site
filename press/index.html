04The longer story500 words

DEcoDLE isn't a word game and isn't a rhythm game — it's both. The Wordle-likes of the post-2022 daily-puzzle shelf are almost entirely re-skinned word lists; DEcoDLE takes the format in a direction the daily-puzzle space hasn't seen: every guess attempt must be earned, not just typed.

The result is a hybrid that hasn't existed before. Each guess attempt is gated by an Acoustic Cypher — a quick beat-matching round where the player taps symbols in time with a generated rhythm track. Players have unlimited attempts to complete the Cypher; a clean run unlocks powerful bonus rewards, while a sloppy completion still advances the puzzle — just without the Perfect Sync bonuses on the line.

The puzzle keeps Wordle's structural clarity: six attempts, a five-symbol sequence drawn from an eight-symbol alphabet, color-coded feedback after each guess. The rhythm layer adds a second skill dimension and an entirely different texture to the daily run. Players who like Wordle for the deduction puzzle still get it; players who like rhythm games for the embodied timing get an additional reward loop. Players who like both — a smaller but genuinely served audience — finally have a game built for them.

DEcoDLE ships with three modes. Daily mode is a single shared puzzle that resets at midnight UTC. Endless mode runs continuous puzzles for skill-building and grinding the in-game grade system. Challenge mode lets players encrypt their own five-symbol sequence into a shareable DC-XXX code and send it to a friend — a social vector that turns the game into a creator surface as well as a consumer one.

The game offers three difficulty tiers. Casual mode keeps the Cypher gate forgiving — rhythm-game novices and players who prefer the puzzle over the timing still get full puzzle rewards, full Perfect Sync availability, and full access to the in-game economy. Hard mode tightens both the puzzle and the rhythm window. Expert mode is reserved for the highest-skill runs — human completions are rare. The Diamond-tier grade — FLAWLESS.EXE — is reachable on any difficulty through a perfect-timing run.

DEcoDLE is the first release from Ad Astra Vanguard Studios LLC, a California indie studio founded in 2026. The Acoustic Cypher mechanic is patent-pending under two USPTO filings — #64/037,214 and #64/061,463, both filed in 2026. The game is built on React Native and Expo, a deliberate technology choice the studio is happy to discuss with developer-press outlets.

Around the game runs DEcoDLE: The Journal Logs, a six-week serialized narrative campaign told as a back-and-forth correspondence between two in-game AI characters, :/AI+> and :/System>. The drip runs 120 episodes on X (@DEcoDLE_app) and culminates in a print companion of the same name, released June 12, 2026.

DEcoDLE is free with an optional $2.99 PRO upgrade, available worldwide on the App Store and Google Play.

05The mechanic — one-page explainer

DEcoDLE's central innovation is the Acoustic Cypher, the patent-pending mechanic that gates every guess attempt behind a rhythm round.

The Cypher Keyboard.An eight-symbol grid — all eight symbols available from the start. The daily target is a hidden 5-symbol sequence drawn from those eight symbols.
The Cypher.A short rhythm pattern generated for each guess attempt — up to five symbol-and-beat pairings on a rhythm track, scaling by difficulty (Casual starts at 2, Hard and Expert start at 3, capped at 5 by late rounds). The player must tap the indicated symbol on the keyboard at the indicated beat for each pairing.
The Gate.Clear the Acoustic Cypher and the next row of the Puzzleboard unlocks. The player enters a five-symbol sequence guess from the keyboard onto the Puzzleboard; the game returns Wordle-style color-coded feedback (symbol in correct position, symbol present but wrong position, symbol absent). Six Puzzleboard rows maximum per run.
Perfect Sync.Land every symbol in one clean cycle and the player earns bonus rewards — Power-Ups deployable during later Puzzleboard or Acoustic Cypher rounds. The Bitflip pool includes Binary Cypher Sight, Early Hint Reveal, AI+ Assist, Sync Dividend, and others. Firewall Breach is Expert-tier only.
The Grades.A six-tier in-game grading system rewards both puzzle solving and rhythm accuracy. From bottom: KEEP TRYING · DEVELOPING · SOLID · BRILLIANT · ELITE · FLAWLESS.EXE. The top tier is reachable through perfect-timing or one-shot solves.
Difficulty.Three tiers — Casual, Hard, Expert — modulate both the puzzle constraints (symbol-pool size, repetition rules) and the rhythm timing window. Casual mode is designed for puzzle-first players who want the daily with a forgiving rhythm gate. Expert mode is rare; human completions are notable.
Coming in v1.1.0 — Acoustic Cypher Evolved (Evo mode).A second visual surface for the Cypher arrives in the first feature-tier release after the 1.0.x stability line. The 8-symbol keyboard stays the same — what changes is the Cypher display itself: Classic shows each beat's target symbol cycling through a static center monitor; Evo replaces that monitor with a Cypher Dial whose target symbol rotates around a radial track, changing position as well as identity per beat. Same scoring, same rewards, same patent-pending core mechanic — a different visual rhythm to read. Players will be able to switch between Classic and Evo at will. The side-by-side coexistence model is covered by the second USPTO provisional (#64/061,463, filed 2026-05-08).
